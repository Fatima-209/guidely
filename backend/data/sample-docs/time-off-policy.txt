# Time Off Policy

## Requesting time away
Submit planned vacation requests in the People Portal at least ten business days before the first day away. Your manager approves or declines requests in the portal. Wait for approval before booking non-refundable travel.

## Sick leave
Notify your manager in Slack as soon as practical on the first day of illness. Record sick time in the People Portal when you return. For absences longer than three consecutive workdays, contact People Operations about documentation requirements.

## Holidays
The company holiday calendar is published in the People Portal each January. Part-time schedules follow the employee handbook.
