# Remote Work Guide

## Availability
Publish working hours and time zone in the team calendar. Keep your calendar current and set a status when away for more than one hour during normal work hours. Teams may agree on shared collaboration hours.

## Equipment and security
Use company-managed devices and install operating system updates promptly. Connect through the company VPN on public or untrusted networks. Store work files only in approved company storage.

## Expenses
Submit eligible home-office expenses through Finance within 30 days, with an itemized receipt and manager approval.
