# Customer Onboarding Guide

## Kickoff
After a signed order form, the account owner schedules a kickoff within five business days. Confirm goals, stakeholders, timeline, integrations, and success measures. Record decisions in CRM account notes.

## Workspace setup
Create the customer workspace from the approved template. Assign an administrator and verify domain ownership before inviting users. Send the getting-started guide and explain how to contact Support.

## Launch readiness
Confirm required data is imported, integrations are tested, and the customer administrator has completed training. Record launch approval in CRM. Escalate blocked criteria to the implementation lead.
